# Build the shared chassis


Power stays off

Place the printed chassis flat. Match motor clamps to the intended holes; teacher confirms screw sizes (TBD).

Fit the two motors symmetrically. Route wires clear of shafts. Fit the 65 mm wheels without bending shafts.

Attach the caster. Check that both wheels touch a level surface and spin without rubbing.

Mount ESP32 and L298N with insulated spacing. Keep the driver heatsink clear.

Secure the switched battery holder so cells cannot contact conductive parts.

Fit the mini breadboard and route jumpers with slack but away from wheels.

For line following, fit two downward-facing modules. For avoidance, fit three facing left/front/right. Do not connect a sensor output to two GPIOs.

Mechanical checklist

No loose wires under wheels.

No exposed terminals touching chassis hardware.

Switch reachable; USB socket accessible.

Teacher checks fasteners and battery installation.
