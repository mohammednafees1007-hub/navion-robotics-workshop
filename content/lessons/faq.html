Do I need a phone?

No. Use your Windows 10/11 laptop. Phones are not part of this workshop.

Are these three separate robots?

No. One shared chassis changes sensor arrangement and program between experiments.

Can the website drive it?

No. Commands travel through USB or an assumed working Bluetooth Classic COM connection.

Can I use any ESP32?

The remote sketch needs the original ESP32 with Classic SPP. Confirm the supplied WROOM-32 board and exposed GPIOs.

Is the certificate official accreditation?

No. It records completion of this workshop's configured learning requirements.

Where do I get help?

Ask your workshop teacher. Support contact and post-workshop schedule are TBD. Bring your test log, sketch version and exact error.

After the workshop

Review official Arduino and Espressif documentation with an adult. Keep the original wiring map and record every modification. Do not change battery charging arrangements as a student experiment.
