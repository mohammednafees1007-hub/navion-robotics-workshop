What makes a robot?

A robot senses its surroundings, makes a decision, and acts. Our IR modules sense; the ESP32 follows your program; the motors act. A remote robot also listens to a human.

Your workshop journey

  1. Check the kit and learn safe handling.
  2. Assemble one chassis and upload a serial test.
  3. Drive with a Windows laptop over USB, then try Bluetooth Classic.
  4. Move two sensors underneath to follow a line.
  5. Move three sensors to the sides and front to avoid obstacles.
  6. Test, improve, and explain your result.

Read a lesson, perform its checklist with your teacher, answer its quiz, then mark it complete. The portal teaches and provides downloads; it does not control the robot. Grades 6–10 are the working audience, pending confirmation.
